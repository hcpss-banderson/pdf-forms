school students who require the services of a licensed childcare provider within
the service area for the districted school on a daily basis. **Please note that
forms submitted for the upcoming 2025-2026 school year will not be processed
until after September 15th.**

1. Space Available School Bus Service may be terminated on very short notice if space is no longer available on the bus.
2. The Office of Student Transportation will be responsible for determining available space on each bus.
3. Additional school bus stops will not be added, and students will be expected to utilize an existing bus stop.
4. The childcare provider's license number must be included on the form.
